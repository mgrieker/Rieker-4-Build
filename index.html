Velociraptor

One of the most recognizable of the herbivorous dinosaurs, the Triceratops lived around 68-65 million years ago. Estimated to weigh about 6-12 tons, Triceratops was around 26-29.5 ft long and 9.5-9.8 ft tall. They are easily recognized due to the three horns protruding from their brow and snout, as well as the frill at the back of their skull.

Triceratops

The Velociraptor is among some of the most recognizable dinosaurs. It was a bipedal carnivore known for its speed and agility. Modern research has provided evidence that they were feathered, a hypothesis that has been applied to several other species of dinosaur. Velociraptors could run at about 65 mph, were up to 6.8 ft tall, weighing from 15-33 lbs, and lived about 75-71 million years ago.

Tyrannosaurus Rex

Arguably the most famous of the dinosaurs, the T-rex is a staple of pop culture. The T-rex live around 68-66 million years ago and roamed across what is now North America. It was a bipedal carnivore with a massive skull that was balanced by its heavy tail. Most estimates place the T-rex at weighing approximately 5-7 tons.
